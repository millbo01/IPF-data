# VIX ETP flow — research specification

## Purpose

Test a mechanically forced-flow mechanism with three separate verdicts:

1. **Mechanism validity** — does predictable VIX ETP issuer demand move the receiving market in the expected direction?
2. **Indicator usefulness** — does the signal improve another strategy as a filter, state variable, sizing input, or timing input even if it is not independently profitable?
3. **Standalone tradability** — can a directly traded rule clear realistic costs, sizing constraints, and the passive-equity hurdle?

A failure of (3) must **not** be recorded as a failure of (1) or (2).

## Mechanism

Leveraged and inverse VIX ETPs target a fixed daily multiple of a short-term VIX-futures benchmark. Because their NAV and futures exposure move during the day, they must mechanically rebalance exposure near the close. Market makers receiving the VIX-futures flow can hedge through SPX options and SPX/ES futures, creating a potential transmission channel from predictable VIX-futures demand into end-of-day equity-index returns.

For leverage multiple beta and prior NAV/AUM L, the leverage-rebalancing component generated by an underlying benchmark return r has first-order notional magnitude proportional to:

`beta * (beta - 1) * L * r`

Calendar roll and creations/redemptions are separate components.

## Existing evidence

This is not being treated as a novel mechanism discovery.

- Bangsgaard & Kokholm (2025), *Journal of Banking & Finance*, “The stock market impact of volatility hedging: Evidence from end-of-day trading by VIX ETPs”, DOI 10.1016/j.jbankfin.2025.107556: daily VIX-futures demand by VIX ETP issuers predicts end-of-day SPX futures returns in- and out-of-sample, with evidence of subsequent reversal.
- Brøgger (2022), *Journal of Banking & Finance*, “The market impact of predictable flows: Evidence from leveraged VIX products”, DOI 10.1016/j.jbankfin.2021.106280: leveraged VIX-product rebalancing measurably affects late-day VIX-futures prices; impact is subsequently reversed and larger/more predictable flows attract liquidity provision rather than simple predatory front-running.
- Bangsgaard & Kokholm (2024), *Journal of Financial Markets*, “The lead–lag relation between VIX futures and SPX futures”, DOI 10.1016/j.finmar.2023.100851: VIX futures lead SPX futures particularly when volatility is high; relative liquidity and hedging activity affect the lead–lag relation.

The research objective is therefore practical: determine whether the flow can be reconstructed cheaply and whether it has useful incremental information for the IPF trading system.

## Data policy

Do not spend QuantConnect tick-history time until the public-data feasibility layer passes.

Start by archiving public daily holdings/AUM for the major short-term VIX ETPs:

- ProShares: UVXY (+1.5x), VIXY (+1x), SVXY (-0.5x)
- Volatility Shares: UVIX (+2x), SVIX (-1x)

Sources:

- ProShares daily holdings CSV: `https://accounts.profunds.com/etfdata/psdlyhld.csv`
- ProShares historical NAV CSV: `https://accounts.profunds.com/etfdata/historical_nav.csv`
- Volatility Shares public product pages for UVIX and SVIX

The archive is prospective and append-only. Public source data may be stored in `IPF-data`; licensed QuantConnect/AlgoSeek raw market data must not be committed.

## First decision gate

Before any heavy backtest, establish:

1. the holdings/AUM inputs can be captured reliably without paid data;
2. aggregate mechanically implied rebalance demand is occasionally economically material relative to VIX-futures liquidity near the close;
3. the signal can be calculated before the proposed equity-index trade window closes.

If these fail, standalone trading is deprioritized, but the flow may still be retained as an indicator/state variable if it can be observed cheaply.

## Later testing hierarchy

Only after the public-data layer is stable:

- reconstruct daily ETP demand;
- test incremental predictive content versus plain VIX/VX return, term structure, volatility level, and recent ES return;
- keep **indicator tests** separate from **standalone PnL tests**;
- if standalone economics survive, test an executable fine-grained venue appropriate to small capital before building a CME-futures implementation.

No parameter optimization is authorized at this stage.
