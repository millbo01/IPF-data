## Important sizing correction
The first-stage probe used £200 annualised P&L volatility per market as a conservative coarse screen. That is not the final frozen MAIN allocation.

From the frozen MAIN implementation:
- `M_MAIN = {T1: 1.4335, F: 4.54643, Dip: 3.64361}` at a 10% portfolio-vol target.
- Target fraction per market is `sum_k M_k * w_k / nlive_k`.
- Each active book weight has 10% single-market volatility before portfolio scaling.

At £2,000 and the full frozen universe this implies approximately:
- T1: £13.03 annualised P&L volatility per live market (22 markets).
- Dip: £33.12 per active market (22-market universe).
- Forced continuation: £53.49 per active eligible market (17-market universe).

These are the relevant component-level granularity references, not £200 per market.

## Venue classification
**IG TRANSLATION: PARTIAL PASS / ECONOMIC RERUN WARRANTED.**

The original CME failure mode (whole-contract granularity making nearly everything zero or wildly oversized) is materially reduced on IG, but not eliminated. The authenticated 0.01 stake is fine enough for many MAIN component exposures and too coarse for several volatile commodity legs. Because the three books net into one market position, component-level pass counts are not sufficient to judge the combined engine.

The decisive next test is an unchanged-rule historical MAIN rerun with:
1. the frozen T1/F/Dip signals and M_MAIN multipliers;
2. IG quote-unit transforms per market;
3. target spread-bet stake rounded to the authenticated 0.01 minimum/increment, with sub-minimum targets rounded to zero rather than deliberately oversized;
4. observed IG futures/forward spreads and conservative 2× spread-cost stress;
5. the original build/holdout split and no signal/weight retuning;
6. £2,000 starting capital, margin monitoring, compounded return, realised volatility, max drawdown and comparison with the unrounded MAIN model.

No additional candidate discovery is justified before this translation test is adjudicated.
